# Hackathon 2027 planning

Welcome to the planning for the 2027 Digital Earths Global km-scale Hackathon.

The hackathon is currently scheduled for **June 7-11, 2027** in [several locations](docs/nodes) around the globe.

If you are interested in hosting a node, please feel invited to do so and have a look at the draft state of the [technical concept](docs/technical_concept). We already have several [model teams](docs/models) planning to contribute data. If you want to contribute a simulation, please have a look at the draft [data request](docs/data-request.md), and the [technical concept](docs/technical_concept.md)
